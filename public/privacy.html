Middle Path Coach

Privacy Policy

Effective October 7, 2026

Middle Path Coach is an AI coaching companion offered by Chris Holmberg dba Middle Path Consulting ("Middle Path," "we") to clients of Middle Path coaches. This policy explains what information the service handles, where it goes, and what you can do about it. The short version: we don't keep your conversations, we don't sell anything, and we don't track you.

What we collect and keep

  • Account information, entered by your coach when your access is set up: your name, optionally your email address, your team (if any), and your license end date.
  • Usage records: how much of your allowance you've used, how many sessions you've started, and when you last used the coach.
  • Your personal access link, which works like a password for your account.

What we don't keep

Your conversations and any report you upload pass through our server so the coach can respond, but we don't store them. Your current conversation is saved only in your own web browser, on your device, until you start a new session or clear your browser data. If you want a copy, use "Download conversation."

Who handles your information

  • Anthropic provides the AI model. Your messages and any uploaded report are sent to Anthropic to generate each response. Anthropic's commercial terms prohibit training its models on this content. Anthropic deletes it within a limited period (currently 30 days), except where it must keep it longer for safety or legal reasons.
  • Vercel hosts the site and our server. Like most hosts, it keeps basic technical logs (such as IP address and time of request) for security and operations. These logs don't include your conversation content.
  • Upstash stores your account information and usage records.
  • Your coach (Middle Path, or the Middle Path certified coach who set up your access) can see your account information and usage records. Your coach cannot see your conversations unless you choose to share them.

We don't sell or rent your information, and we don't share it for advertising.

How we use it

We use your account information and usage records only to run the service: to check your access, apply your allowance and usage limits, keep the service secure, and contact you about your account.

Cookies and tracking

The site uses no cookies, no analytics, and no advertising. All files, including fonts, are served from our own site, so no other company collects information about your visits through it. Your browser's local storage holds your access link and your current conversation so they survive a page reload. Because we don't track you across websites, we don't change anything in response to "Do Not Track" browser signals. There is nothing to turn off.

How long we keep it

We keep account information and usage records for the length of your license and for a reasonable period afterward for record-keeping. You can ask us to delete them sooner, though that ends your access.

Your choices

  • Ask your coach to see, correct, or delete your account information.
  • Clear your conversation at any time with "New session" or by clearing your browser data. On a shared computer, download anything you want to keep and then clear it.
  • Don't share information that is legally privileged or that you're not allowed to share with third-party services.

Who the service is for

Middle Path Coach is for adult professionals who are clients of a Middle Path coach. It is not intended for anyone under 18.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll post the new version here with a new effective date. If a change is significant, we'll also let you know through your coach or by email.

Contact

For any question about this policy or your information, contact your coach or Middle Path Consulting.
